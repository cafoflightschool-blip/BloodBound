RULES

Community rules cover Discord. Game servers have their own tabs.

Admins reserve the right to remove anyone disrupting the server atmosphere or gameplay. These are the general Blood Bound Community Rules and apply across the entire Discord. Game-server rules are in the Squad and WARDOGS tabs.
Rule 1.1 — Be Respectful

Always be respectful towards others. Discussions, arguments and banter are fine, just keep it reasonable. Personal attacks, targeted harassment or repeatedly trying to provoke other members will not be tolerated.

Rule 1.2 — No Spamming or Advertising

No spamming, advertising or recruiting. This includes message spam, mic-spam, advertising or self-promoting streams, YouTube channels, clans, Discord servers etc. Permission to promote can be obtained from Clan Management.

Rule 1.3 — No Inappropriate Content

Do not post pornography, graphic gore, malicious links, scams, illegal content or anything that violates Discord’s Terms of Service or Community Guidelines.

Rule 1.4 — No Trolling

Banter and jokes are welcome, but intentionally disrupting channels, repeatedly targeting members or creating problems for the Community is not.

Rule 1.5 — No Discrimination

Zero-tolerance towards racism, homophobia, sexism or any other form of serious discriminatory behaviour or harassment.

Rule 1.6 — No Politics

No references to political, extremist or controversial groups, movements or incidents, current or historical. Examples: Azov, Wagner, IDF, Iran, Hamas, Nazis, KKK.

Rule 1.7 — No Doxxing

Do not share another person’s private or identifying information without their permission. Threats, blackmail or attempts to expose another member’s personal information will result in serious action.

Rule 1.8 — Minimum Age

The minimum age for this Community is 16. Members must also comply with Discord’s Terms of Service.

Rule 1.9 — No Impersonation

Impersonating admins, members, developers, Discord staff or other Community staff will be harshly punished.

Rule 1.10 — Admin decision

To appeal an admin decision or complain about staff, use the relevant Contact an Admin channel and follow the instructions there.

Admins reserve the right to remove anyone disrupting the server atmosphere or gameplay.

Server rules

2.1 No Teamkilling
  • No intentional TKs for any reason.
  • Apologise for TKs in ALL chat.
2.2 No Ghosting
  • No ghosting or stream sniping. Do not share intel with the enemy during the game.
  • Examples: stream sniping, saying GG during an active round, baiting for intel.
2.3 No Exploits

Exploits and cheats are not allowed.

2.4 No Main Camping
  • Main base camping is not allowed.
  • Attacking mortar FOBs, UB32s, TOWs or chasing a vehicle back is allowed. Then return to active objectives. Attacking people leaving main is not allowed.
2.5 Play the Objective
  • All squads play active objectives.
  • Commanders should use role assets (UAV, artillery, etc.).
2.6 Squad Leader Requirements
  • SLs speak English, use a mic and communicate.
  • Only create a squad if you will lead it.
  • SLs can kick anyone.
  • General infantry squads need 4 members to lock.
  • Do not kick people under 4 just to keep the squad for friends.
2.7 Specialised Infantry Squads
  • Can lock below 4 if clearly named: MORTAR, CMD, Tow-FOB, LOGI.
  • Logis are a team asset. Coordinate.
2.8 Vehicle Claims
  • Specialised vehicle squads can lock below 4. One claim per squad. Name the asset in the squad name.
  • Open-top gunner seats such as MRAP/MK19 are not specialised vehicles.
  • Examples: BTR82A, Simir Kornet, M2A3, M1A1, UH60M.
  • First correctly named squad gets the claim. Commander loses vehicle claims when they take CMD.
  • Unclaimed vehicles are team assets. Claim ends when the vehicle dies or returns to main.
  • APC, IFV, MBT, HELI, BRADLY are not valid claim names.
  • Crewman-kit vehicles must have 2 crewmen in driver/gunner.
2.9 No Asset Hogging
  • Don’t hog vehicles. Let others try.
  • Console commands to claim vehicles are discouraged.
2.10 No Asset Wasting
  • No abandoning vehicles or heli ramming. IED bikes are allowed.
  • Don’t waste tickets on purpose at the end of a game.
  • Clipping rotors of other helis counts as ramming.
2.11 Bot kicks
  • Steam account under 10 days.
  • No Steam Community profile.
  • Game or VAC ban in the last 180 days.
  • Name under 3 characters, hidden/special characters, URL in name, under 3 Latin characters.

2.1 Is it okay to teamkill if…?

Never. Even if they steal your claimed vehicle. Record it and report it in Contact an Admin.

2.2 Can I stream on Discord?

Not the live game on our Discord. Use a 15 minute delay on Twitch / other Discords if you can.

Can I say GG at the end of an active round?

No. That intel changes how the enemy plays.

Can I switch teams mid-round?

Not after you have played on the other team. Instant switch after joining is fine.

2.3 Glitched under the map?

Do not shoot people. Respawn. Do not share info from the glitch.

2.4 Mines near enemy main?

Not on roads out of main unless it is an active objective. That is main camping.

TOW 800m+ from their main watching logis?

Yes, that is still main camping.

Looking into main to spot vehicles?

That is main camping, regardless of distance.

Hunting the Grad / mortar vic — can I shoot other vics?

Only engage the Grad/mortar vic or mortar FOB. You may shoot back if they shoot first.

If you are in doubt, you are main camping.
2.7 Valid locked infantry squads under 4

CMD/COMMAND. LOGI — no exclusive claim on logis. MORTAR — keep it small (3–4). TOW — no exclusive claim on logis.
