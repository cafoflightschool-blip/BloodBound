ABOUT

Quick Introduction

Blood Bound was originally a Project Reality clan created by PR veterans BB | Hudson "Really Evil" and ArkUTD. Since the Clan's humble origins, it has been rebuilt from the ground up, reinvigorated with a new breath of life, and has grown steadily. Over the past years BB has transformed from an underdog into one of Squad’s most successful communities and one of Squad’s most popular servers.

The players wearing the BB tags are a group of like-minded individuals who have come a long way from a shared past that ties us together. BB is continually expanding, and we are always looking out for those people who play as a team, have the skill and mindset of a serious Squad player — but who also know how to have a bit of fun. We pride ourselves on our ability to balance good, coordinated team-play along with a good-natured and respectful sense of humor.

Outside of Squad we also play an array of games, with members free to enjoy whatever they want to play. Our Discord is the heart of Blood Bound. This is our corner of the net where we hang out and play games with the rest of the world, and we welcome everyone to our Community.
