SQUAD RULES

Rules, Rules FAQ, then the vic claim list.

General Server Seeding Rules FAQ Vic claim list

Admins reserve the right to remove anyone disrupting the server atmosphere or gameplay.

1.1 Be Respectful

Keep discussions civil. No offensive language, personal attacks or harassment.

1.2 No Spamming or Advertising

No mic-spam, advertising or recruiting without permission from Clan Management.

1.3 No Inappropriate Content

No pornography, racism, “feet”, discriminatory speech, bright flashing images or gore.

1.4 No Griefing

No trolling, griefing or toxicity.

1.5 No Discrimination

Zero-tolerance for racism, homophobia, sexism or any discrimination.

1.6 No Politics

No political or extremist references in names or chat. Examples: Azov, Wagner, IDF, Iran, Hamas, Nazis, KKK.

1.7 Appealing a Ban

Use the Rules FAQ below.

1.8 Minimum Age

16.

1.9 No Impersonation

Impersonating admins, members or OWI staff will be harshly punished.

2.1 No Teamkilling
  • No intentional TKs.
  • Apologise for TKs in ALL chat.
2.2 No Ghosting
  • No ghosting or stream sniping.
  • No saying GG during an active round or baiting for intel.
2.3 No Exploits

Exploits and cheats are not allowed.

2.4 No Main Camping
  • Main camping is not allowed.
  • You may deal with mortar FOBs, UB32s, TOWs or chase a vehicle back, then return to active objectives.
2.5 Play the Objective
  • All squads play active objectives.
  • Commanders should use role assets.
2.6 Squad Leader Requirements
  • English, mic, communicate.
  • Only create a squad if you will lead it.
  • General infantry needs 4 to lock.
2.7 Specialised Infantry Squads

Can lock below 4 if named MORTAR, CMD, Tow-FOB or LOGI. Logis are a team asset.

2.8 Vehicle Claims
  • One claim per squad. Name the asset in the squad name.
  • Open-top gunners such as MRAP/MK19 are not specialised vehicles.
  • First correctly named squad gets the claim. Commander loses claims when they take CMD.
  • Unclaimed vehicles are team assets. Claim ends when the vehicle dies or returns to main.
  • APC, IFV, MBT, HELI, BRADLY are not valid names.
  • Crewman-kit vehicles need 2 crewmen in driver/gunner.
2.9 No Asset Hogging

Don’t hog vehicles.

2.10 No Asset Wasting

No abandoning vehicles or heli ramming. IED bikes are allowed.

2.11 Bot kicks

Steam under 10 days, no community profile, VAC/game ban in 180 days, short names, hidden characters, URL in name.

If the server is under 60 players, seeding rules are in effect until a live broadcast.

3.1 Seeding FOB placement

Don’t build FOBs / HABs on middle caps.

3.2 Don’t hurt seeding

Don’t camp map borders and kill people crossing into the play area.

RULES FAQ

Is it okay to teamkill if…?

Never. Report it in Contact an Admin.

Can I stream on Discord?

Not the live game on our Discord. Use a 15 minute delay elsewhere if you can.

Can I say GG during an active round?

No.

Can I switch teams mid-round?

Not after you have played on the other team.

Glitched under the map?

Don’t shoot. Respawn. Don’t share intel from the glitch.

Mines / TOW / looking into main?

If the only purpose is watching people leave main, it is main camping. If you are in doubt, you are main camping.

Valid locked infantry under 4

CMD, LOGI (no exclusive logi claim), MORTAR, TOW.

How do I appeal / report?

Contact an Admin. Do not DM staff unless asked. Tag Squad Admins, give Steam permalink, in-game name, team/squad, what happened, video if you have it. If an admin said no, tag Independent Admin Affairs.
