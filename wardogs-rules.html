WARDOGS RULES

Vanilla and Hardcore. Same Community standard.

RulesRules FAQ

Admins reserve the right to remove anyone whose behaviour significantly disrupts the Community, gameplay or safety of others — even if it is not listed below.

Rule 1 — Be Respectful

Discussions are allowed. Keep them civil. No personal attacks or harassment. Banter at the enemy is fine until it becomes harassment or discrimination.

Rule 2 — No Spamming or Advertising

No loud music, ads or recruiting without Clan Management permission. Stream names in-game are fine if they follow the rules.

Rule 3 — No Inappropriate Content

No porn, racist content, excessive gore, or flashing images meant to harm.

Rule 4 — No Griefing

No trolling, throwing or toxicity aimed at teammates. Applies on Discord and the servers.

Rule 5 — No Cheats or Exploits

Cheats, hacks, scripts, exploits, glitches or unfair third-party tools are banned.

Rule 6 — No Discrimination

Zero-tolerance for racism, sexism, homophobia, religious discrimination or hate.

Rule 7 — No Politics

No political arguments or extremist references in chat, names or tags. Examples: Azov, Wagner, IDF, Iran, Hamas, Nazis, KKK.

Rule 8 — Appealing a Ban

Use the Rules FAQ. Do not DM admins unless they ask.

Rule 9 — No Impersonation

Impersonating Blood Bound admins/members or Bulkhead staff will be harshly punished.

RULES FAQ

FAQ-1 Contact an Admin

Report players, appeal a ban, or ask something that needs an admin.

FAQ-2 How do I report?

Players: Contact an Admin. Tech: technical / bug channels. Gameplay: feedback channel.

FAQ-3 DM admins?

No, unless they ask.

FAQ-4 Appeal

Tag Admin, Steam permalink, why the ban should change.

FAQ-5 Voice reports?

No. Text only.

FAQ-6 SteamID64

Copy the Steam profile URL, paste it into steamid.io, copy the steam64ID.

How to report
  • Tag Admin
  • In-game name
  • Team / squad
  • What they did
  • Video if you have it
