WARDOGS RULES

Vanilla and Hardcore.

RulesReport / Appeal FAQ

Admins reserve the right to remove anyone whose behaviour significantly disrupts the community, gameplay or safety of others; even if the behaviour is not explicitly covered by the following rules.

Rule 1: Be Respectful

Always be respectful towards others. Discussions and disagreements are allowed, just keep them civil.

Do not engage in personal attacks, harassment or abusive behaviour. Friendly banter and casual shit-talk towards the enemy team is allowed, provided it does not cross into harassment or discrimination (see Rules 6, 7).

Rule 2: No Spamming or Advertising

Excessively loud music, advertising and self-promotion; including streams, YouTube channels, Discord servers, clans or other communities, is not allowed without permission from Clan Management.

In game usernames may include your YouTube or Twitch name, provided they otherwise follow the server rules.

Rule 3: No Inappropriate Content

Do not post, share, or distribute inappropriate or disturbing content.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content
  • Racist, discriminatory, or derogatory content
  • Excessive gore or disturbing imagery
  • Bright flashing images or content intended to cause discomfort or harm
Rule 4: No Griefing

Do not intentionally ruin the experience of other players.

This includes trolling, griefing, intentionally throwing games, or excessive toxicity intended to negatively affect teammates.

Everyone is expected to participate in good faith and contribute to a respectful gameplay environment. This rule applies to both Discord and our game servers.

Rule 5: No Cheats or Exploits

Cheats, hacks, scripts, exploits, glitches or third-party software that provide an unfair advantage are strictly prohibited.

Rule 6: No Discrimination

Discrimination of any kind is strictly prohibited.

We have zero-tolerance for racism, sexism, homophobia, religious discrimination, or any other form of discriminatory or hateful behaviour.

Rule 7: No Politics

Political arguments, propaganda, extremist content, or attempts to provoke political conflict are not permitted within the community.

References in usernames, clan tags, or other in-game identifiers are also prohibited.

No references to Political, Extremist or Controversial groups, movements or incidents; whether Current or Historical.
e.g "Azov." "Wagner." "IDF." "Iran" "Hamas." etc

No references to current or Historical political or extremist groups.
e.g Nazis, KKK etc

Rule 8: Appealing a Ban

Go to #wardogs_report_appeal_faq for this info.

Rule 9: No Impersonation

Impersonating admins, members of Blood Bound or Bulkhead staff will be harshly punished.

REPORT / APPEAL FAQ

Admins reserve the right to remove anyone disrupting the server atmosphere or gameplay. We may timeout or ban people who spam or chat needlessly in #wardogs_contact_an_admin

FAQ-1: What is contact an Admin used for?

Use #wardogs_contact_an_admin to directly contact our admin team to report problematic players, appeal a ban, or ask questions you think might need a server admin's attention.

FAQ-2: How can I report issues?

Use #wardogs_contact_an_admin for issues with players, #tech_talk_and_support for technical issues and #wardogs_feedback for gameplay related feedback.

FAQ-3: Can I contact admins directly?

Please do not DM admins unless explicitly asked to as part of handling an issue with another player. Report all issues in #wardogs_contact_an_admin

FAQ-4: How do I appeal bans?

If you are here to appeal a ban, go to #wardogs_contact_an_admin, tag @Wardogs Admin Full and give us your Steam profile permalink and a statement regarding your ban, and why you feel we should considering lowering or removing your ban.

FAQ-5: Can i get help in voicechat?

No we refrain from using voicechats for reports, and keep all the reports in the #wardogs_contact_an_admin

FAQ-6: How to get the SteamID64 from a Steam profile

Steam Desktop

  1. Go to the Steam profile page
  2. Right-click anywhere on the page. Select Copy Page URL
  3. Go to steamid.io
  4. Paste the page url from step 2 in the search bar in the middle of the page. Click lookup.
  5. Click Copy to clipboard to the right of steam64ID. Paste where needed.

Steam in browser

  1. Go to the Steam profile page
  2. Copy the URL
  3. Open steamid.io in a new tab
  4. Paste the page url from step 2 in the search bar in the middle of the page. Click lookup.
  5. Click Copy to clipboard to the right of steam64ID. Paste where needed.
Guide for making a report in #wardogs_contact_an_admin

When reporting a player, please

  1. Tag the @Wardogs Admin Full.
  2. Provide the ingame name of the offending player, so we can identify them.
  3. Tell us what team they're on, and if possible, in which squad.
  4. Explain in detail what the player did.
  5. If possible, provide any relevant evidence, such as footage of the incident or relevant screenshots - video evidence being preferred.
