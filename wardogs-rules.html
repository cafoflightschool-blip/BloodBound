WARDOGS RULES

These rules apply on Blood Bound WARDOGS servers (Vanilla and Hardcore) and in related Discord channels.

Rules Rules FAQ

RULES

Admins reserve the right to remove anyone whose behaviour significantly disrupts the Community, gameplay or safety of others — even if the behaviour is not explicitly covered by the following rules.
Rule 1 — Be Respectful

Always be respectful towards others. Discussions and disagreements are allowed, just keep them civil.

Do not engage in personal attacks, harassment or abusive behaviour. Friendly banter and casual shit-talk towards the enemy team is allowed, provided it does not cross into harassment or discrimination (see Rules 6 and 7).

Rule 2 — No Spamming or Advertising

Excessively loud music, advertising and self-promotion — including streams, YouTube channels, Discord servers, clans or other communities — is not allowed without permission from Clan Management.

In-game usernames may include your YouTube or Twitch name, provided they otherwise follow the server rules.

Rule 3 — No Inappropriate Content

Do not post, share, or distribute inappropriate or disturbing content. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content
  • Racist, discriminatory, or derogatory content
  • Excessive gore or disturbing imagery
  • Bright flashing images or content intended to cause discomfort or harm
Rule 4 — No Griefing

Do not intentionally ruin the experience of other players. This includes trolling, griefing, intentionally throwing games, or excessive toxicity intended to negatively affect teammates.

This rule applies to both Discord and our game servers.

Rule 5 — No Cheats or Exploits

Cheats, hacks, scripts, exploits, glitches or third-party software that provide an unfair advantage are strictly prohibited.

Rule 6 — No Discrimination

Discrimination of any kind is strictly prohibited. Zero tolerance for racism, sexism, homophobia, religious discrimination, or any other hateful behaviour.

Rule 7 — No Politics

Political arguments, propaganda, extremist content, or attempts to provoke political conflict are not permitted.

References in usernames, clan tags, or other in-game identifiers are also prohibited.

No references to political, extremist or controversial groups, movements or incidents — current or historical. Examples include Azov, Wagner, IDF, Iran, Hamas, Nazis, KKK, and similar.

Rule 8 — Appealing a Ban

Use the Rules FAQ below. Do not DM admins unless they ask you to.

Rule 9 — No Impersonation

Impersonating admins, members of Blood Bound, or Bulkhead staff will be harshly punished.

RULES FAQ

Admins reserve the right to remove anyone disrupting the server atmosphere or gameplay. Do not spam reports.
FAQ-1 — What is Contact an Admin used for?

Report problematic players, appeal a ban, or ask a question that needs a server admin.

FAQ-2 — How can I report issues?

Player issues: Contact an Admin. Technical issues: the technical / bug channels. Gameplay feedback: the gameplay-feedback channel.

FAQ-3 — Can I contact admins directly?

Do not DM admins unless explicitly asked. Report all issues in Contact an Admin.

FAQ-4 — How do I appeal a ban?

Go to Contact an Admin, tag the Admin role, give your Steam profile permalink, and explain why the ban should be lowered or removed.

FAQ-5 — Can I get help in voice chat?

No. Reports stay in Contact an Admin, not voice.

FAQ-6 — How to get a SteamID64

Steam desktop: open the profile → right-click the page → Copy Page URL → paste into steamid.io → lookup → copy steam64ID.

Browser: copy the profile URL → paste into steamid.io → lookup → copy steam64ID.

How to make a report
  • Tag the Admin role.
  • Provide the in-game name of the offending player.
  • Tell us their team, and squad if you know it.
  • Explain in detail what they did.
  • Add evidence if you have it. Video is preferred.
